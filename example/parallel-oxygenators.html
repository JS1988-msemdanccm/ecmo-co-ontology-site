Clinical Scenario

V-V ECMO — Refractory Hypercapnia, Second Oxygenator Added in Parallel

Adult patient with severe ARDS. Initial single-oxygenator V-V circuit fails to achieve adequate CO₂ clearance despite maximum sweep gas flow. A second membrane oxygenator is added in parallel via a Y-connector on the post-pump limb, doubling the effective membrane surface area.

Epoch 1 (2026-05-08 12:00 to 2026-05-11 09:15 UTC): standard single-oxygenator V-V; FemV drainage → Pump → Oxy_A → IJ return
Epoch 2 (from 2026-05-11 09:15 UTC): parallel configuration added; post-pump flow splits at Y-piece to Oxy_A and Oxy_B, recombines at downstream Y-piece before return cannula

Both epochs share the V-V mode. The ELSO Registry records each membrane lung with its own start and end time and a concurrent-use flag, but does not capture how concurrent membrane lungs are connected (in series or in parallel).

Key ECMO-CO Features in This Scenario
Two CannulationConfiguration epochs on one ECMORun — same ECMORun individual; Epoch 1 ends at 2026-05-11T09:15:00Z, Epoch 2 begins at the same time. Both epochs are about the same patient and circuit individuals.
Circuit aggregate with >1 Oxygenator — ecmo-co:ECMOCircuit bfo:0000178 lists both Oxy_A and Oxy_B as members. Epoch 1 config ecmo-co:isAbout only Oxy_A; Epoch 2 config ecmo-co:isAbout both — capturing when each oxygenator entered the active configuration.
Parallel section via CircuitConnection — four ecmo-co:CircuitConnection GDC records asserted by Epoch 2: Y→OxyA, Y→OxyB (upstream split), OxyA→Y, OxyB→Y (downstream recombine). The two split connections leave the same post-pump Y-piece through its two outlet ports, each port part of (bfo:0000176) that Y-piece. The connections cover the parallel section only: the pump and cannulae carry no Ports in this scenario, so the path to and from the patient is not represented. The ELSO Registry does not capture port-level connections.
Mode alone does not describe the circuit — the Epoch 2 rdfs:comment reads: "Same mode (V-V); circuit topology changes from serial to parallel oxygenators. Demonstrates that mode-classifier alone is insufficient to characterise circuit configuration."

SPARQL Competency Queries

Q1 — Oxygenators per configuration epoch of a run

# Namespace declarations omitted.
SELECT ?epoch ?start (COUNT(DISTINCT ?oxy) AS ?oxygenators)
WHERE {
  ?run    ecmo-co:hadConfigurationEpoch  ?epoch .
  ?epoch  ecmo-co:startedAt  ?start ;
          ecmo-co:isAbout    ?oxy .
  ?oxy    rdf:type  ecmo-co:Oxygenator .
}
GROUP BY ?epoch ?start
ORDER BY ?start
?epoch | ?start | ?oxygenators ---------------------|------------------------|------------- :Config_POX01_Epoch1 | "2026-05-08T12:00:00Z" | 1 :Config_POX01_Epoch2 | "2026-05-11T09:15:00Z" | 2

Q2 — Retrieve the circuit connections asserted by a configuration epoch

# Namespace declarations omitted.
SELECT ?cfg ?conn ?upstream ?downstream
WHERE {
  ?cfg   ecmo-co:assertsConnection  ?conn .
  ?conn  ecmo-co:hasUpstreamPort    ?upstream ;
         ecmo-co:hasDownstreamPort  ?downstream .
}
ORDER BY ?conn
?cfg | ?conn | ?upstream | ?downstream ---------------------|---------------|-----------------------|--------------------- :Config_POX01_Epoch2 | :CC_OxyA_to_Y | :Port_OxyA_out | :Port_PostOxy_Y_in_A :Config_POX01_Epoch2 | :CC_OxyB_to_Y | :Port_OxyB_out | :Port_PostOxy_Y_in_B :Config_POX01_Epoch2 | :CC_Y_to_OxyA | :Port_PostPump_Y_outA | :Port_OxyA_in :Config_POX01_Epoch2 | :CC_Y_to_OxyB | :Port_PostPump_Y_outB | :Port_OxyB_in

All four connections of the parallel section are asserted by the Epoch 2 configuration: the split at the post-pump Y-piece and the recombination at the post-oxygenator Y-piece.
