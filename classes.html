About this reference

ECMO-CO is a realist, top-down domain ontology: every class sits under a class of Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) 2020. FMA is a SKOS annotation mapping, not an import. Physical kind is modelled separately from the role/function a thing bears. Placement follows these BFO rules, and where ECMO-CO is stricter than BFO the tables mark the rule as ECMO-CO policy:

  • BFO: a relational quality depends on two or more bearers at once, none a spatial region and no two sharing a part (BFO 2.0 Reference [057-001], [131-004]).
  • BFO 2020: a quality or a realizable entity (role, disposition, function) inheres in an independent continuant that is not a spatial region (BFO 2020 inheres in, 051-BFO; realizable entity, 058-BFO). The BFO 2020 OWL file sets no material-bearer requirement for a quality or a role, and its role examples include the role of a boundary (058-BFO, 061-BFO).
  • BFO 2.0 Reference, stricter for roles: every realizable entity, a role included, specifically depends on some material entity ([063-002]), and that material entity is then a bearer of it ([051-002], [053-004]).
  • BFO: a function is a disposition, and a disposition's bearer is a material entity (BFO 2.0 Reference [062-002], [064-001]).
  • ECMO-CO policy, narrower than BFO because it names the bearer classes: drainage and return roles, like drainage and return functions, are borne only by material cannula parts (LumenConduit or CannulaTip), never by a Site; circuit blood pressure is borne by the blood, never by a Site; the two contact interfaces take material bearers.

The tables below give the Phase 1 TBox in full.

Scope: v0.1 defines 36 classes across the two ontology files (31 ready + 5 pending), 10 object properties, 2 datatype properties, and three mode classifier individuals, checked by a four-layer test pipeline (OWL parse → OWL-RL closure → SHACL → SPARQL competency queries). curationStatus on each row marks whether a class is finalized (ready) or defined in v0.1 but not yet finalized (pending) — a curation-review state, distinct from the Phase 2 roadmap layers (SOSA, PROV-O, QUDT, IAO), which are proposed and shown dampened at the bottom.

Instantiation. Of the 5 pending, CircuitConnection and ConfigurationMode are used in the Phase 1 scenarios (ConfigurationMode through its three mode individuals). The other 3 (RecordedConnection, FenestrationRim, PatientCircuitAssembly) are forward-declared Phase 2 stubs — declared in the ontology but not instantiated in any scenario file.

Continuant — Immaterial entity (Site) BFO:0000029
CannulaLumen, CannulaFenestrationSite BFO:0000029Immaterial entities — three-dimensional, bounded by material (BFO site; BFO 2.0 Reference [034-002]). Not spatial regions: BFO 2020 declares site and spatial region disjoint subclasses of immaterial entity, so a Site is an independent continuant that is not a spatial region, and the BFO 2020 OWL file would let it bear a quality or a role. ECMO-CO policy does not let a Site bear a role or a function. For a function this is also a BFO requirement (a function is a disposition, whose bearer is material — BFO 2.0 Reference [062-002], [064-001]). For a role it is ECMO-CO policy relative to the BFO 2020 OWL file; the BFO 2.0 Reference would itself require a material bearer ([063-002]). The SHACL role-bearer shape enforces this and the negative fixture tests it. For qualities, see the interface rows below: ECMO-CO declines a Site as bearer of the contact interfaces and of circuit blood pressure, but requires Site bearers for SpatialContinuityInterface.

Continuant — Specifically dependent continuant (quality / realizable)
CircuitBloodPressureQuality BFO:0000019A quality that inheres in a portion of circuit blood and that is the pressure that portion of blood exerts at the place in the extracorporeal circuit it occupies at a given time. Its bearer is a PortionOfCircuitBlood, never a Site — ECMO-CO policy (BFO would permit a Site bearer), enforced by a SHACL shape. It carries no measured value; observations are Phase 2.
PhysicalInterface — CircuitInterface, DeviceTissueInterface, SpatialContinuityInterfaceRelational quality BFO:0000145The genus of blood-relevant physical interfaces. Depends on ≥2 bearers; per BFO a bearer may be any independent continuant that is not a spatial region, no two sharing common parts (BFO 2.0 Reference [057-001], [056-002], [131-004]) — not required to be Ports or even material. Ceases when the interfacing relationship ends. All four ready; the three subclasses are pairwise disjoint, and bearer materiality is set per subclass:
• CircuitInterface — Port↔Port mating (the only class carrying the two-Port rule);
• DeviceTissueInterface — cannula↔tissue apposition (material bearers, no Ports);
• SpatialContinuityInterface — Site↔Site open blood-conducting continuity (two immaterial Site bearers, e.g. a fenestration and the atrial cavity — no Ports, no material). Material bearers on the two contact subclasses are ECMO-CO policy (physical contact requires surfaces), not a BFO requirement; the Site-bearer requirement on the continuity subclass is machine-enforced.
DrainageRole, ReturnRoleRole BFO:0000023Realizable; the configured use of a bearer in an epoch.
DrainageFunction, ReturnFunctionFunction BFO:0000034Realizable; the design intent of a lumen conduit (distinct from its current role).
